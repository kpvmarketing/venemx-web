Our Story

Where Two Cultures Share the Table

Venemex Bakery & Café was born from a simple, powerful idea: Venezuela and Mexico share a passion for bold flavors, warm hospitality, and food made from the heart. We are a family-owned Venezuelan restaurant with a Mexican soul — and we bring both traditions together under one roof, right here in Daytona Beach.

We serve breakfast and lunch six days a week. Come for Venezuelan food like golden tequeños (cheese sticks), arepas, empanadas and pabellón criollo, Mexican food like tacos, burritos and quesadillas, and freshly brewed coffee with handcrafted pastries and cakes from our bakery — every bite is a journey across two countries, and every visit feels like coming home.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

✦

Where can I find Venezuelan food in Daytona Beach?

At Venemex Bakery & Café, 201 Seabreeze Blvd, Daytona Beach, FL. We're a Venezuelan and Mexican restaurant serving arepas, empanadas, tequeños, patacones and pabellón criollo for breakfast and lunch, Tuesday through Sunday (closed Mondays).

What are tequeños?

Tequeños are Venezuelan cheese sticks: white cheese wrapped in dough and fried until golden. Order them as a side (6 pieces) or in our Venemex Combo for 2.

Do you offer takeout or online ordering?

Yes. Order online for pickup or delivery through DoorDash with the Order Now button on this site, order on Uber Eats, or call us at (386) 265-0055.

Where do I park?

Free parking is available on the right side of the building at 201 Seabreeze Blvd.

Do you have vegetarian options?

We don't have a dedicated vegetarian menu, but several items are vegetarian-friendly — the arepa de queso, empanada de queso, quesadilla de queso, and sides like tequeños, plantains and french fries.

Do you take reservations or host private events?

We don't take regular table reservations, but the restaurant can be booked for private events. We also offer catering — call us at (386) 265-0055 to plan your menu.
